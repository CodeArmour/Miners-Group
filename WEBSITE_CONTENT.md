# Miners Group — Website Content & Product Brief

## 1. Website Purpose

The Miners Group website is the official introduction to the group, its mission, goals, services, programs, and community.

The website should communicate that Miners Group is more than a programming community. It is an ecosystem that connects learning, mentorship, practical experience, real software projects, internships, and career development.

The primary journey is:

**Learn → Practice → Build → Get Mentored → Gain Experience → Work**

The website should make visitors quickly understand:

- What Miners Group is.
- Why Miners Group exists.
- Who it is for.
- What services and opportunities it provides.
- How students and developers can join.
- How companies and organizations can work with Miners Group.
- How mentors and partners can contribute.

## Why Miners Exists

Traditional learning often focuses on syntax, frameworks, and isolated exercises.

Professional software development requires much more:

- Working with other developers.
- Understanding requirements.
- Planning implementations.
- Using version control correctly.
- Reviewing code.
- Testing software.
- Communicating technical decisions.
- Fixing problems found during QA.
- Documenting work.
- Taking responsibility for delivery.

Miners Group is designed to expose members to this wider engineering process.

# 14. FAQ SECTION / PAGE

## What is Miners Group?
Miners Group is a technology community and software organization focused on learning, practical experience, mentorship, software projects, internships, and career development.

## Do I need professional experience to join?
No. Miners Group should support different experience levels. Some programs and projects may have specific prerequisites.

## Is Miners Group only for students?
No. Students are an important part of the community, but developers, mentors, companies, universities, and technology professionals can also participate.

## Can I apply for an internship?
Yes. Internship opportunities should be announced when teams and suitable projects are available.

## Does Miners Group provide software development services?
Yes. Miners Group can work with companies, startups, organizations, universities, and entrepreneurs on software projects.

## Can experienced developers become mentors?
Yes. Experienced developers should be able to apply to mentor members, lead workshops, or contribute to projects.

## Are all programs free?
Miners Group may provide a combination of free community resources and paid professional programs or services. Pricing should be clearly communicated for each program.

## Can universities or companies partner with Miners Group?
Yes. Partnerships can include training, internships, events, projects, mentorship, sponsorships, and talent initiatives.

# 17. Tone of Voice

The website should sound:

- Ambitious
- Modern
- Professional
- Welcoming
- Practical
- International
- Technology-focused
- Student-friendly without sounding childish

Avoid exaggerated startup claims such as "revolutionizing the world" or claiming achievements, customers, member counts, partnerships, or statistics that do not exist yet.

Miners Group should communicate ambition while remaining credible.

---

# 18. Important Content Rules for the Implementation Agent

1. Do not invent statistics such as member counts, completed projects, customers, countries, or success rates.
2. Do not invent partner/company logos.
3. Do not invent testimonials.
4. Do not invent team members.
5. Do not invent social-media URLs.
6. Clearly mark features/programs that are not launched yet as "Coming Soon" when necessary.
7. The main focus of the first website version is introducing Miners Group and explaining the vision, goals, services, and opportunities.
8. Prioritize clear storytelling over filling the site with unnecessary sections.
9. Every major page should have a meaningful CTA.
10. The website must serve both sides of Miners Group: people seeking growth opportunities and organizations seeking technology/collaboration.
11. Do not present interns as unsupervised client developers. Professional delivery and mentorship should remain central to the message.
12. Keep content in reusable data structures/components where appropriate so programs, projects, FAQs, and opportunities can later come from a CMS or backend.

# 20. Final Message to the Website Agent

Build Miners Group as an organization with a clear mission, not as a generic software-agency landing page.

The central story is the connection between **education, practical experience, mentorship, real projects, and opportunity**.

A visitor should leave the website understanding this sentence:

> Miners Group helps people move from learning technology to gaining real software experience, while also bringing developers, mentors, companies, and organizations together to build useful technology.

The website should make Miners Group feel like the beginning of a serious international technology ecosystem that can grow over time without pretending that future ambitions already exist today.
